# ReelBite Hollywood — rules
- English true-Hollywood-story Shorts, 55-70 s (never over 3 min). Audience: US, UK, Australia, Europe adults. Not made for kids; comments on, moderation Strict.
- 2-3 Shorts a day (07:00, 12:00, 17:00 IST), one in production at a time. Anand uploads (Private/Scheduled, public after ~2 h).
- Facts: every story is grounded in its English Wikipedia article (saved as wiki.txt in the project). The script may only use facts from that text; a fact-checker compares every line and card with it (one rewrite, then Anand). Rumours are never stated as facts. Quotes word for word, attributed.
- Real proof on screen: people only from their own Wikidata photo; places/objects from Wikimedia Commons (free licences, credited); headline/quote cards with the source; charts and big-number cards. Comic panels only where no real image exists. Photo labels never claim a year or place the photo is not from.
- No faked faces or voices; no trailer/film footage fetched by the Studio. Anand's own short clips: put them in the project's your_clips folder named by beat (b05.mp4 = beat 5) — the editor cuts in up to 6 s, muted.

## The edit (top-editor standard, 26 Oct 2026)
- Shorts spec: 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 High, AAC-LC 48 kHz stereo, MP4 faststart, loudness -14 LUFS.
- Safe zone: everything between y=210 and y=1480 and left of x=920 (YouTube's top bar, right-hand buttons and bottom title area stay clear). Logo small at the top right of the safe zone.
- Hook (0-3 s): bold hook title top-centre, a boom on the first frame, the most shocking fact first, starting mid-action.
- A new picture every ~1.5-2 s: 1-3 shots per beat; inside a photo shot a hard punch-in on the subject or full-bleed half-way; white flash + shake on cuts.
- Punch lines: short stamp text ("$44 MILLION.") slammed in at the end of 5-7 beats, with a hit sound.
- Captions: 1-2 words at a time, Arial Black, white with black stroke; numbers/money red, names neon yellow; centred in the safe zone.
- Pop-ups: comic words, emojis, arrows pointing at the subject.
- Jump cuts: voice at 1.15x, every pause over 0.12 s removed, 0.05 s between lines.
- Seamless loop: the last line leads straight back into the first.
- Sound: the Studio's own music bed (tension / mystery / upbeat, made by code — no copyright), ducked under the voice; whoosh on cuts, camera shutter on photos, pop on cards, glitch on charts, boom on the hook, hits on punch lines.
- Outro: end card with logo + "Follow for daily Hollywood stories" inside the last line, so the loop still works.


## v3 design (Anand, 26 Oct 2026)
- Full screen: hi-res photos and b-roll fill the phone; small pictures get a comic frame over their own blurred copy. Cards, charts and punch lines sit on the beat photo or footage, never plain black.
- Cinema tools: Pexels b-roll (red carpet, theatre, film set, projector) between proof shots, illustrative only and credited; animated cinema graphics (flashes, marquee, clapperboard, curtain spotlight, film strip, reel).
- End: question + logo + SUBSCRIBE button tapped (click + bell) + for more movie facts / legend stories.
- Stories alternate movie story -> legend actor biography. Playlists: Movie Facts and Actor Facts.
- Length 60-90 s (9-13 beats, 170-240 words at 1.15x).
