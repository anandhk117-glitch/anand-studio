# Putani Loka Kids — channel rules (never change without Anand)

- Language: pure, simple Kannada. Clear pronunciation.
- Audience: kids 2–8 and parents. Channel is Made for Kids. Category: Education.
- Visual style: 3D Pixar / Cocomelon style, 4K, bright festival colours. The style sentence goes FIRST in every image prompt. No photorealism.
- World: Karnataka village — red-tiled roofs, coconut trees, mud roads, rangoli, jaathre, temples, paddy fields.
- Characters: Kumar, Lakshmi, Raju, Meera + the elephant mascot. Always use the reference sheets. Never redesign. Only Raju wears the red-gold Mysore peta; Kumar has big curly black hair and nothing on his head; Lakshmi two short braids with red ribbons and jasmine; Meera one long braid. All four same height, normal slim build — write "NOT chubby, NOT fat" in every prompt. Exactly one Aane. No text or labels in scene pictures. Same body shapes in every shot.
- Camera logic must match the scene (in a classroom the kids face the board; the camera sits at the board for their faces).
- Make 360° character and location reference sheets for every song before scenes.
- Shots: 5–10 seconds each, never longer than 10 s. Movement or camera change every shot.
- Proven winners: elephant, cow/animal and jaathre/festival songs; traditional canon (Govina Gundu, Punyakoti). Festival songs from a kid's-eye view beat pure mythology.
- Same 4–6 s brand intro on every video.
- Kids rules: no comment prompts, no end screens, no subscribe asks. Nothing scary, violent or unsafe on screen.
- Every song ships as one full video + 3 Shorts + full upload package (title, description, tags, thumbnail).
- Publish anchor: Saturday 7:00 AM IST.


## Edit (the editor agent follows this)
- Every clip is fitted to its lyric slot at 0.9×–1.12× speed — never slow motion, never reversed. Hard cuts on lyric starts.
- A scene longer than one clip gets two shots: another take of the same picture, or a punch-in — never a frozen still.
- Pictures fill the whole 16:9 frame (no black bars); small clips are upscaled and sharpened.
- Karaoke line at the bottom in Kannada, logo bottom-right, clean brand intro (no subscribe/share badges), warm end card.

## Retention (what keeps kids watching)
- Hook in the first 5 seconds of the song: the hero animal/kid big on screen with a sound or a surprise.
- Movement or a camera change every shot (3–6 s); an energy shift about every 45 s (new place, dance, counting, surprise).
- A small story problem in the middle (lost calf, missing toy) and a happy fix — the mid-song peak.
- Repeat the chorus at least 3 times; the last chorus is the biggest.

## Upload package (the publisher agent follows this)
- 3–4 title options, Kannada first + emoji | English | Kannada Kids Song(s) YEAR, ≤95 characters, keywords in the first 45.
- A curiosity title (question/surprise) usually beats a plain topic title.
- Description: Kannada hook + keywords, the same in English, chapters, what kids learn, "More from Putani Loka Kids", exactly 3 hashtags.
- 25–35 tiered tags under 480 characters. No comment or subscribe asks.

## Thumbnail (the thumbnail designer and the weekly thumbnail doctor follow this)
- One big hero with a big happy or surprised face, bright warm colours, 2–4 big Kannada words in yellow with a dark outline, never over a face.
- Always make an A/B second thumbnail with different words for YouTube "Test & compare".
- Our best: First Day of School — 10.2% CTR, 1:15 average view. Goal: every video ≥ 8–10% CTR. Weak ones get a new thumbnail from the doctor every Sunday.


## Channel trademark (Anand, 22 Oct 2026)
- Signature chorus dance "Putani Kunita" (ಪುಟಾಣಿ ಕುಣಿತ): 1 Putani Clap (clap twice above head), 2 Aane Sway (arm as trunk, sway left-right), 3 Kunita Hop (hands on hips, two hops), 4 Hello Wave (both hands wave). Same order in every chorus of every song.
- Every instrumental break gets a hook, rotating: A reaction close-up (surprised/laughing face), B Aane flaps ears and waves trunk, C all kids clap on the drum beats.
- No 3-second hook at the start; the full original Putani Loka intro plays first.
