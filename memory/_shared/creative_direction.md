# Creative direction playbook — kids song channels (Putani, Bumbu, Sparkle)
The agents follow this from idea to upload. Goal: a big-channel (Cocomelon-level) song video a child wants to watch again — never "AI slop".

## 1. Song design (songwriter)
- One clear story a 3-year-old can follow: setting → fun → small problem (lost calf, missing toy) → search → happy fix → thank-you / goodbye.
- Structure: Intro (sound hook in the first 5 s: animal sound, bell, a name called) → Chorus → Verse 1 → Chorus → Verse 2 (call and response) → instrumental fill (counting or dance) → Chorus → Verse 3 (problem) → Resolution → Final Chorus (bigger) → Outro.
- Chorus 4 short lines, repeated at least 3 times, the same words every time. Echo words in brackets ("(ಅಂಬಾ!)") so kids sing back.
- Every verse line is a picture: a WHO + a VERB + an OBJECT that can be shown ("Ajji gives warm milk"). Avoid abstract lines.
- Leave 3–4 s music fills after counting lines and between sections — they become hook moments.
- Energy changes about every 45 s. Target 3:00–4:00. Suno style: the channel's style string, clear pronunciation, no EDM.

## 2. Shot plan (visual director)
- Lyric times are HEARD from the song before planning. One scene = one clip (max 5.8 s), or a continuous shot of 2–3 clips.
- Read every line: list who must be seen, what they do, which object/animal. The plate (picture) must contain exactly those.
- Story with the sound off: each beat of the story is visible in order.
- Shot sizes follow the music: wide group shots for choruses, medium for verses, close-up reaction on a surprise or question, something special in every music break.
- Never the same action in neighbouring shots. Every next shot moves the story on. Only the chorus signature dance repeats (fixed order).
- Calm / instrumental / slow passages: continuous shots — the next scene "continue_previous", starting from the last frame of the previous clip (2–3 clips = one 10–15 s shot), each part continuing the action.
- Music breaks get a hook, rotating: big reaction close-up → mascot cute move → kids clap on the drums.
- 18–25 plates for a 3–4 min song; a plate is used at most 4 times, each time with a different action.

## 3. Picture prompts (image agent)
- Order: style sentence → CHARACTER LOCK from the sheets (each kid's exact hair and clothes, word for word from the wardrobe) → place and which side we see → who stands where, left to right → action/pose → camera → "Exactly N kids", "small 5-year-old Pixar children, big round heads, NOT teenagers, NOT chubby" → "no text, letters, numbers or labels" → "16:9".
- Leave open space between kids and big animals/props. Animals full size with normal legs.
- Anything to count or read is added in the edit, never drawn.
- Check every picture before any clip: count, faces, clothes, peta only on Raju, sizes, ages, camera side, no text. Remake failures.

## 4. Motion prompts (choreographer)
- Start with a 3–5 word label. ONE clear action per clip that acts out the lyric's verb with what is in the picture.
- Slow, smooth, cinematic: gentle continuous movement with a LOCKED camera (no zoom, no camera move — zooms make the clip tools morph faces and animals; the editor adds the gentle drift). Fewer, smaller movements look cleaner than big actions. Fast energy only in the chorus dance.
- Everyone stays on their own spot unless the line says they come/run; nobody passes behind or through anything; objects move only when a hand moves them; animals do one small natural action and keep their legs on the ground unless the line says they jump.
- Faces, hair, clothes, ages and sizes never change. No new characters, animals, objects or text.

## 5. Clips (motion director + clip checker)
- Takes are checked before use: identity, warping, passes-through, backwards jumps, flying objects, body/age change, blur, too still (motion < 1.25), camera zoom cutting people off.
- Spare takes are reused only for the same action. The liveliest clean take wins.

## 6. Edit (editor)
- Full brand intro → song with hard cuts on the beat inside a section, smooth transitions between sections → song plays to its last note → brand outro full screen.
- Karaoke line at the bottom only; big numbers on screen for counting lines; logo bottom-right; 24 fps, smooth interpolation for choppy clips, gentle camera drift (none across a continuous shot), bright kids grade.

## 7. Director review (before Anand sees it)
- Watch every scene against its lyric (using the lyrics' own names — a named calf is a calf). Unclean clips are remade (up to 2 rounds); a scene that does not show its line goes back to the visual director with what it must show.

## Coordination
Songwriter → (audio, heard lyric times) → visual director (plates + shot plan + motion prompts, using this playbook and the channel rules) → image agent → picture check → clips → clip check → editor → director review → packaging (titles, thumbnail, Shorts). Each agent reads the channel rules, lessons and Anand's decisions first. Anand's corrections are final and become lessons.
