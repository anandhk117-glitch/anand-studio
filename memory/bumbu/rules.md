# Bumbu Ki Duniya — channel rules (never change without Anand)

- This is NOT Putani Loka. Never use Kannada lyrics, Putani characters or Karnataka folk instruments.
- Language: simple everyday Hindi (Devanagari). All upload copy bilingual: full Hindi + full English.
- Audience: kids 2–8 + parents, India + NRI. Made for Kids.
- Visual style: 3D Pixar-style animation, 4K, warm golden-hour light, bright colours, North Indian village / festive world. Style sentence FIRST in every prompt. No photorealism.
- Story formula: small problem → friends help → sing and dance resolution.
- Runtime: 2:15–2:35 (the channel's proven length).
- Music: North Indian folk balgeet — dholak, manjira, harmonium, bansuri, warm female lead + kids chorus, call-and-response, 115–125 BPM. No EDM / western pop / autotune.
- Cast (locked looks, never redesign): Bumbu (baby elephant), Chidiya Rani (bird with tiara), Bandar Mama (monkey in red vest). Costume changes only, never redesign.
- Shots 5–10 s (Grok 6 s or 10 s clips).
- Thumbnails: close-up expressive character face, bold rounded Hindi + English text, warm golden/orange, dark outline. No clickbait.
- Kids rules: no comment prompts, no "comment below", nothing scary, violent or unsafe.
- Upload: first confirm YouTube Studio shows Bumbu Ki Duniya, not Putani Loka.
- Bumbu pose per shot (Anand, 28 Oct 2026): walking/running/eating/sleeping on all four legs; talking/singing/dancing/waving/hugging/giving standing upright on hind legs, cartoon style. Fixed sets: one empty location picture per place, every shot there matches it.
