# Sparkle Kids Planet — channel rules (draft — Anand to confirm)

- Language: simple English for toddlers.
- Audience: kids 2–6 worldwide + parents. Made for Kids. Category: Education.
- Visual style: 3D Pixar-style animation, 4K, bright candy colours. Style sentence FIRST in every prompt. No photorealism.
- Characters: not locked yet — upload character sheets in Brand, then list them here. Never redesign once locked.
- Songs: short lines, lots of repetition, one learning idea per song, actions kids can copy.
- Shots 5–10 s.
- Kids rules: no comment prompts, nothing scary, violent or unsafe.
- Keep fully separate from Putani and Bumbu assets.
