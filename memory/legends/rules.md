# Legends Decoded — channel rules

- Global English documentary channel: the mindset and decisions behind historical and contemporary figures.
- Audience: adults in the UK, US and Europe (high RPM). NOT made for kids.
- Brand: midnight navy and gold; Cinzel (titles) + Montserrat (body); narration voice "The Decoder".
- Cadence: one episode every 15–30 days — quality over speed.
- Launch slate: Gandhi (script done), Ronaldo, Messi, JFK, Ali, Jobs, Mandela, Einstein, Bruce Lee, Kobe.
- Facts must be accurate. Mark anything unsure (verify) and check before recording.
- Real people: respectful portrayal, no invented quotes presented as real, no likeness used to imply endorsement.
- Thumbnail formula: subject portrait left; 2–3 line title right with one gold-highlighted word; pillar tag (MINDSET / POWER / CHAMPION / VISIONARY); brand bug top corner.
- Title formulas: "The Untold Story of…", "Inside [X]: The Most Powerful…", "The Tragedy of…". SEO anchor: success mindset.
